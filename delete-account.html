Delete your account and data

App: PlateJack · Developer: PlateJack

The short version

  • You can delete your PlateJack account and its data yourself, inside the app.
  • Can't use the app? Email platejacksupport@gmail.com from your account's email with the subject Delete my account.
  • Deletion is permanent and can't be undone.

Option 1: Delete in the app

  1. Open PlateJack and sign in to the account you want to delete.
  2. Go to Settings › Account & Data.
  3. Under Danger zone, tap Delete account.
  4. Type DELETE to confirm, then tap Delete my account.

Your account and synced data are deleted from our servers immediately.

Want a copy of your data first? Export a backup in Settings › Data & Backup before you delete.

Option 2: Ask us by email

Send an email to platejacksupport@gmail.com:

  • From: the email address you signed up with, so we can verify the account is yours.
  • Subject: Delete my account

We will confirm and delete your account, synced data, feedback and push subscriptions within 30 days of a verified request.

Email a deletion request

What gets deleted

  • Your login and account profile, including your email address.
  • All data synced to your account: food log, workouts, lifts, goals, weights and the rest of your app data stored with PlateJack.
  • Feedback you sent and push notification sign-ups.
  • When you delete in the app, the data PlateJack saved on that device is erased too.

What may be kept, and for how long

  • Backups held by our hosting provider expire on their normal schedule.
  • Data saved only on a device (when you use PlateJack without an account) never reaches us. It stays until you erase it in Settings › Data & Backup › Erase all data, clear your browser storage or uninstall the app.
