Getting started

Do I need an account?

No. PlateJack works without an account. Your food log, goals, plans and settings are saved on your device.

Why create an account?

An optional account backs up your data and syncs it across your devices. Sign in with the same account on each device. To sign up, open Settings, tap the top card, then Sign in or create account.

How do I install it on Android?

Open PlateJack in Chrome, tap the menu, then Install app or Add to Home screen.

How do I add it to my iPhone Home Screen?

Open PlateJack in Safari, tap the Share button, then Add to Home Screen. Open it from the new icon.

Is PlateJack medical advice?

No. Calorie, nutrient and cost numbers are estimates for general wellness tracking. Talk to a doctor or registered dietitian before big diet or exercise changes.

Logging food

How do I log a meal?
  1. Open Add and pick the meal: Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner or Snacks.
  2. Search for a food. Your own foods show first, then the USDA and Open Food Facts databases.
  3. Tap a result, choose the serving or grams, then tap Log.
I can't find my food.

Tap Custom food and type the numbers from the nutrition label.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Logging, your foods, recipes, plans and the built-in food database work offline. Live food search and sync wait until you are back online.

Why are food costs only estimates?

Prices change by store, city and week. PlateJack uses typical US grocery prices divided by servings. You can edit any ingredient price to match your store.

Snap a plate & photo privacy

How does Snap a plate work?

Take or choose a photo of your meal. PlateJack sends it to Google's Gemini AI to identify the foods and portions, then shows you the results to check and edit before you log them. Snap a plate needs you to be signed in.

What happens to my food photos?

PlateJack does not store the photo. It is passed to Google's Gemini AI to identify the food. PlateJack uses Gemini's free tier, so Google may use these photos to improve its products. Don't use Snap a plate for photos you don't want shared with Google.

Can I log without sending a photo?

Yes. Choose Pick the foods myself and log by picking foods manually. Your photo stays on your device.

Photo scan limit

How many photo scans do I get?

Free accounts get 3 Snap a plate scans a day.

I hit the limit. Now what?

You can keep logging by searching or picking foods manually, with no limit. Your scans are available again the next day.

Workouts and your calorie goal

Do workouts change my calories for the day?

Yes. When you log a workout, PlateJack adds calories to today's goal. By default it adds half of the calories burned, because workout estimates often run high.

Can I count all of my burned calories?

Yes. Open Settings and find Workout calories added to goal. Choose Full to count all of them, or Half (recommended) to go back.

Account & data

Where is my data stored?

Without an account, your data is saved on your device only. If you sign in, a copy is also stored in your private account so it can sync. We don't sell your data and we don't show ads.

How do I export my data?

Go to Settings › Data & Backup and export a backup (JSON) or your food log (CSV).

How do I delete my account?

Go to Settings › Account & Data › Delete account, type DELETE to confirm, and tap Delete my account. This can't be undone, so export a backup first if you want a copy. Can't get into the app? See Delete your account and data.
