Privacy Policy

The short version

  • PlateJack works without an account. Your food log, goals, plans and settings are saved on your device.
  • If you create an optional account, we store your email and a copy of your app data so it can sync. Passwords are handled and hashed by our provider, Supabase; we never see them.

1. Who we are

PlateJack (“PlateJack”, “we”, “us”) is a calorie, nutrition and meal-prep app available at houstonrc.github.io/calorie-tracker and as an installable web app. This policy explains what information the app handles and why.

2. Information we collect

a) Stored only on your device (no account needed)

Everything you enter is saved in your browser’s local storage on your device (under the key ct_data_v1): food entries and portions, calories and nutrients, optional meal photo thumbnails, water, weight and body details you choose to enter (such as age, height, sex, activity level and goals), meal plans, grocery lists, fridge items, saved recipes, budgets and prices, coach and notification preferences, and app settings. Recipe images and app files are cached on your device so the app works offline. We do not receive this data unless you sign in to sync.

b) If you create an optional account

  • Email address and password. Your password is sent securely to Supabase, which stores only a salted hash. We cannot see your password.
  • Synced app data. A copy of the same app data described above (including any meal photo thumbnails you attached) is stored in our database table user_data, linked to your account, so it can sync across your devices. Your demo Pro status is not synced.
  • Account metadata kept by Supabase, such as sign-up and last sign-in times, and technical logs (for example, IP address and request time) used for security and reliability.

c) Feedback you send

If you use “Send feedback”, we store your message, the email you choose to include (optional), your account ID if you are signed in, and the app version in our feedback table. If that fails, the app offers to open your email app instead.

d) Push notifications (optional)

If you turn on push reminders while signed in, we store a push subscription in our push_subscriptions table: the push endpoint and encryption keys provided by your browser, your time zone, your browser’s user-agent string, and your reminder preferences. This lets us send the reminders you asked for.

e) What we do not collect

We do not use advertising IDs, cookies for tracking, analytics SDKs, fingerprinting, or location tracking. We do not collect your contacts, and we do not sell data.

4. Camera and photos

The camera is used only when you choose to scan a barcode or take a meal photo. Barcode reading happens on your device using your browser’s built-in barcode detector; only the resulting number is sent to Open Food Facts. Meal photos are resized on your device and saved as a small thumbnail with your entry. PlateJack does not upload photos for analysis, and photos only leave your device as part of your synced data if you are signed in. You can deny or revoke camera permission in your device settings at any time.

5. Notifications

Reminders (for example, logging, water or fridge items expiring) are optional and off until you enable them. You can change or turn them off in Settings › Notifications or in your device settings. Turning push off removes your push subscription.

6. How we use information

  • To provide app features: logging, plans, grocery lists, budgets, coach tips and reminders.
  • To sync your data when you sign in, and to keep accounts secure.
  • To read and respond to feedback and fix problems.

We do not use your data for advertising, profiling, or automated decisions that have legal or similar effects. Coach tips are generated on your device from your own data.

7. Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share your personal information for advertising. We share data only with the service providers above to run the app, when required by law, or to protect the safety and security of users and the service. If PlateJack is ever transferred to a new owner, this policy will continue to apply to your data, or you will be notified.

8. How long we keep data

  • On-device data stays until you erase it, clear your browser storage, or uninstall the app.
  • Account and synced data stays until you delete it or ask us to delete your account. We delete it within 30 days of a verified request; backups held by our provider expire on their normal schedule.
  • Feedback is kept as long as needed to handle it, and up to 2 years.
  • Push subscriptions are removed when you turn push off, or when they stop working.

9. Your choices: export and deletion

  • Export: Settings › Data & Backup › “Export backup (JSON)” or CSV.
  • Erase on this device: Settings › Data & Backup › “Erase all data”. This permanently deletes the local data on that device.
  • Delete your account and synced data: send a request through Settings › Help Center › Contact / Send feedback (include the email you signed up with), or email platejacksupport@gmail.com with the subject “Delete my PlateJack account”. We will confirm and delete your account, synced data, feedback and push subscriptions within 30 days. You can also sign out at any time.

10. Security

Connections use HTTPS. Database access is protected by row-level security so each account can read and write only its own data. No method of storage or transmission is 100% secure, so please use a strong, unique password.

11. Children

PlateJack is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child under 13 has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

12. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example, under Virginia’s Consumer Data Protection Act, California law, or the GDPR/UK GDPR), you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or get a copy of your personal data, and to opt out of sale, targeted advertising or profiling (we do none of these). To make a request, contact us at platejacksupport@gmail.com. We will not discriminate against you for using these rights. If you are in the EU/UK, our legal bases are performing our contract with you (providing the app and sync), your consent (camera, notifications), and our legitimate interests (security and support). Data is processed in the United States.

13. Health information

Information you enter about food, weight or your body is used only to provide app features for you. PlateJack is not a medical service and is not covered by HIPAA. Calorie and nutrient values are estimates. Not medical advice.

14. Purchases

Pro is currently a free demo unlock with no charge, and no payment information is collected. If paid Pro is offered in the future through the Apple App Store or Google Play, payments will be handled by Apple or Google under their terms, and we will receive only the information needed to confirm your subscription (not your card details). We will update this policy before that happens.
